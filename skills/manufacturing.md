title: 제조와 반도체
description: 공장, 설비, 팹 용어가 많이 나오는 자리
---
The talk is about factories and semiconductor fabs. Use the words Korean manufacturing and fab engineers actually use.
- yield → 수율, fab → 팹, wafer → 웨이퍼, die → 다이, foundry → 파운드리, packaging → 패키징, cleanroom → 클린룸
- tool (in a fab) → 장비, recipe → 레시피, lot → 랏, line → 라인, shop floor → 현장, downtime → 비가동 시간, throughput → 생산량
- predictive maintenance → 예지 보전, defect inspection → 불량 검사, digital twin → 디지털 트윈, takt time → 택트 타임
- Keep acronyms in Latin letters: MES, OEE(설비 종합 효율), PLC, SCADA, EDA, OSAT, HBM, CMP, EUV, SPC, FDC
- In Korean speech, 수율 means yield, 설비 or 장비 means equipment or tool, and 공정 means process (step), not fairness.
