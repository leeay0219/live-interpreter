---
title: 회의
description: 여러 명이 번갈아 말하는 사내 회의나 고객 미팅
---
This is a working meeting, not a talk. People interrupt each other, restart sentences and leave many pieces unfinished.
- Keep subtitles short and plain, like notes a colleague whispers to you. Do not polish half-finished thoughts into speeches.
- Several people speak; never merge two speakers' pieces into one sentence, and never assume who is speaking unless it is said.
- Meeting words stay the way Korean teams say them: 액션 아이템, 팔로업, 오너, 데드라인, 싱크, 얼라인.
- Numbers, dates, prices and customer names are the most important content: never drop or round them.
