---
title: 민감한 대화
description: 불만, 에스컬레이션, 협상처럼 감정이 실린 대화
---
Emotions run high in this conversation, and getting the stance exactly right matters more than sounding smooth.
- Keep each speaker's position and its strength: a complaint stays a complaint, a refusal stays a refusal, a threat to leave stays a threat. Never soften it into agreement and never sharpen it.
- Keep who blames whom exactly as spoken. Never add an apology, a promise, a "we understand" or a compromise that was not said.
- Profanity and insults become the strongest neutral professional words with the same meaning; do not reproduce the words themselves.
- Hedges and conditions (maybe, unless, only if, 일단, 조건부로) must survive the translation.
